# Bloxdy Client roadmap

Updated 2026-11-17. Plans from the project notebook. Scope and priorities may change; no release dates are promised.

The official website is https://rsfire.bloxdyclient.workers.dev.

## Shaders

- **Refine clouds, the sun and lens flare**. Improve their appearance and consistency across lighting and weather.
- **Explore block reflections**. Investigate reflections that fit the existing shader pipeline.
- **Expand world ambience**. Explore four seasons and weather presets for rain, snow, hail and fog.
- **Improve water and lava**. Add varied water waves and controls, more lava looks and settings, and suitable particles.
- **Repair lighting and reported shader bugs**. Prioritize defects found during testing.

## Litematica

- **Make schematics visible and reduce lag**. Fix loading and rendering before adding more tools.
- **Repair paste mode**. Restore placement that currently does not work reliably.
- **Improve building tools and information panel**. Revisit Easy Building and its tower feature, add useful controls, and clarify the panel.
- **Fix issues found by testers**. Use reported cases to guide further repairs.

## Mods and customization

- **Rework Factory Buffer Reuse**. Investigate a broader entity-buffer approach and address reported lag.
- **Customize notifications**. Offer more controls over which notifications appear and repair the current system.
- **Explore a Bloxd SMP market mod**. Show observed average item prices and improve market search, if reliable data is available.
- **Explore mining assistance**. Investigate automatic mining and tool switching for suitable blocks.
- **Explore greater render distance**. Assess feasibility and performance before implementation.
- **Customize the in-game UI**. Explore visibility, position and size controls for individual elements.
- **Save texture and shader pack choices**. Make it easier to reuse selected packs.
- **Improve the Bloxd coding assistant**. Replace the current assistant with a faster, more capable option.
- **Respond to mod requests and bug reports**. Consider ideas from users and testers for new and existing mods.

## Client

- **Improve performance and stability**. Continue measuring and fixing client and mod issues.
- **Explore friends and chat**. Consider messaging and friend features in the launcher.
- **Explore a community space** (under consideration). Investigate social features and possible collaboration with existing communities.
- **Consider a donation option** (under consideration). Explore a voluntary way to support development without making features paid.

## Website and community

- **Keep versions and release notes current**. Publish verified releases and clear records of fixes, additions and known issues.
- **Connect the Discord community**. Add a Discord link when available and share roadmap updates through a dedicated webhook once configured.
- **Consider a custom domain** (under consideration). Investigate bloxdyclient.com without changing the current official address yet.
- **Grow the tester community**. Invite feedback and feature ideas, and make the project easier to discover.

The [release history](https://rsfire.bloxdyclient.workers.dev/versions) records shipped changes separately.
